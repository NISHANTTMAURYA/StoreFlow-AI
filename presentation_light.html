CV Core: Metric BEV Projection & Disjoint Camera Fusion

Overcoming Perspective Parallax and Cross-Camera Blind Spots Without Facial Recognition

MUMBAI SUPERMARKET PROOF: Oblique CCTV feed with YOLOv10 + OC-SORT, yellow ground-contact crosshairs under feet, DPDP 2023 face-masking, and synchronized 2D metric CAD floor plan projection.
Mathematical Formulation
Parallax-Free Ground Contact (H Matrix)
Centroid projection creates 1.2–2.0m parallax error under 45° cameras. StoreFlow AI locates the ground-contact point (u_g, v_g) = ((u_1+u_2)/2, v_max) with ankle keypoints, achieving <0.11m metric accuracy on store CAD coordinates:
s [X, Y, 1]ᵀ = H [u_g, v_g, 1]ᵀ
Geometric Constraint
Zero-Touch Vanishing Point Calibration
Parallel gondola aisle lines intersect at vanishing point v₁; orthogonal floor tile lines define v₂. Applying absolute conic orthogonality v₁ᵀ ω v₂ = 0 automatically computes camera focal length and tilt angle without laser measurement visits.
Zero-PII Re-ID
Camera Link Model (CLM) Fusion
The upper 25% face area is masked in volatile RAM. OSNet-0.5x generates a non-reversible 128-d apparel embedding. Disjoint camera handoffs are constrained by human walking speed (0.8–2.5 m/s), delivering an 86.4% IDF1 score across disjoint views.

Dwell Analytics
Precision Dwell & EPR Engine
Savitzky-Golay smoothing (W=7, p=2) eliminates detection jitter. Transit shoppers (v > 1.1 m/s) are filtered out. 1.0m Voronoi shelf frontages measure the Engagement-to-Passby Ratio (EPR):
EPR = (Shoppers Dwelling ≥ 5s) / (Corridor Passers)
Fluid Mechanics
Crowd Bottleneck Engine
Crowd flow treated as compressible fluid: Accumulation occurs where velocity divergence ∇·v < -τ and density ρ ≥ 1.2 p/m². A Group Deflection filter cancels false alarms from chatting families, while MST queue modeling flags checkout spillovers in 35 seconds.
Layout Optimization
Markov Chain Dead-Zone Discovery
Models store customer circulation via transition matrix Tᵢⱼ = P(Zⱼ | Zᵢ). Spatial Opportunity Score (SOS) pinpoints aisles with <8% discovery rate and simulates staple product relocations to boost circulation.
